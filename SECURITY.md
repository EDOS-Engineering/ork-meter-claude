# Reporting security issues

**Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to `security@edos.io`, or through [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/EDOS-Engineering/ork-meter-claude/security/advisories/new) on this repository.**
Don't open a public issue, pull request or discussion that describes a vulnerability or how to exploit it.

Please include:

- the affected component, version or commit;
- what you expected to happen, and what happened instead;
- a minimal reproduction that uses synthetic data.

**Never send** customer data, prompts, access tokens, API keys, other credentials or payment information, whether in a report, an issue or a commit.

**What we do.** We acknowledge within 3 business days, triage within 5 business days of acknowledgement, and update you at least every 14 days until resolved. We fix Critical issues within 7 days, High within 30 and Medium within 90.

**Scope.** orkmeter.com, go.edos.io, edos.io, orkestera.com, and the Ork Meter plugins and helper. Out of scope: third-party services, denial of service, social engineering, and automated scanner output without a demonstrated impact.

**Coordinated disclosure.** Please allow 90 days from acknowledgement before publishing. We do not pay bounties.

**Safe harbour.** If you follow this policy in good faith, we will not pursue or support legal action against you under the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act, the DMCA anti-circumvention provisions or equivalent laws, and we treat your research as authorised under our terms. This safe harbour covers only Enterprise DevOps Solutions LLC.

Privacy questions and deletion requests go to `edos-labs@edos.io`, not this address.
